Konnextion Real Estate Group, a d/b/a of Konnextion Multi Service Center, LLC ("we", "us"), operates konnextionrealestate.com. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short and in plain English on purpose.

What we collect

  • What you give us. Your name, phone number, email address, property address, and anything you type into the message box when you submit a form on this site.
  • How you found us. If you arrived from a mailer, a text, or a search, the link may carry a short code telling us which one. This helps us know what is worth sending.
  • Basic technical data. Standard web server information such as approximate location, browser type and the pages you viewed. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site trackers on this site.

Why we collect it

To answer you. That is the whole reason. We use your information to prepare your home valuation, respond to your consultation request, and follow up about buying or selling property. If you subscribed to the monthly market note, we use your email to send it.

Calls, texts and emails

When you submit a form, you agree that we may call, text and email you about your property at the number and address you gave us, including by automated means. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text to stop texts, click unsubscribe in any email to stop emails, or simply tell us on the phone and we will take you off the list that day.

Who sees your information

We do not sell your information. We do not rent it. We do not trade it.

It is seen by the people at Konnextion who are working on your request, and by the service providers that make the site run, specifically our form delivery service and our email provider. If a transaction moves forward, we may share what is necessary with the parties you would expect: the brokerage, a title company, a lender, or an attorney. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet real estate recordkeeping requirements, then we delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner and we will, unless a law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

  • Ask us what we have on file for you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you, by any method or all of them.

Email Konnextionbbp@gmail.com or call (754) 226-9387. A person will handle it, not a ticket system.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. No website can promise perfect security, so please do not send Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or copies of identification through the forms on this site. If we need something like that, we will arrange a secure way to send it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of the page.
